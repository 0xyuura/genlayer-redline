# Lanternbox Terms of Service

Version 3. Lanternbox is a fictional file storage service used to demonstrate
Redline. These terms are an example, not a real offer.

## 1. Accounts

An account is required to store files. Keep your password safe; you are
responsible for activity on your account.

## 2. Payment and refunds

Plans are billed monthly in advance. You can ask for a full refund within 7
days of purchase, for any reason, by writing to support or using the in-app
form.

## 3. Your data

Your files remain yours. You can export all of your data whenever you like, in
a standard archive format, free of charge.

## 4. Price changes

We will email you at least 14 days before any price increase takes effect.

## 5. Shared folders

You can now share folders with other Lanternbox users. Shared folders count
against the owner's storage.

## 6. Termination

You may close your account at any time from the settings page.

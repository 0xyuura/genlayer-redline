# Lanternbox Terms of Service

Version 1. Lanternbox is a fictional file storage service used to demonstrate
Redline. These terms are an example, not a real offer.

## 1. Accounts

You need an account to store files. You are responsible for keeping your
password safe.

## 2. Payment and refunds

Plans are billed monthly in advance. Customers may request a full refund
within 30 days of purchase, for any reason, by writing to support.

## 3. Your data

Your files remain yours. Customers can export all of their data at any time,
in a standard archive format, at no charge.

## 4. Price changes

Customers are notified at least 14 days before any price increase takes
effect. The notice is sent by email to the account address.

## 5. Termination

You may close your account at any time from the settings page.
